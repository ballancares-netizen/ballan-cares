About Ballan Cares

Ballan Cares (BC) is a faith-inspired non-profit community development organisation serving vulnerable children, families and communities. Our work focuses on compassion, education, healthcare support, food security, empowerment and sustainable community development.

Our Vision

Transformed communities where vulnerable lives receive hope, dignity and opportunities to thrive.

Our Mission

To serve communities through faith-based compassion and practical solutions addressing social, spiritual and development needs.

Our Programme Areas

We are committed to supporting people and strengthening communities through these areas of service.

Children Support

Supporting vulnerable children through care, protection and opportunities.

Education

Encouraging access to learning and educational opportunities.

Healthcare Support

Promoting community wellbeing through healthcare support.

Agriculture & Food Security

Encouraging sustainable agriculture and stronger food security.

Clean Water

Supporting the aspiration for improved access to clean water.

Elderly Care

Promoting dignity, compassion and support for older people.

Community Empowerment

Encouraging positive participation and sustainable community development.

Youth Development

Creating opportunities for young people to grow and contribute positively.

Ballan Cares Youth Football Initiative

Through football, we encourage young people to develop teamwork, discipline, confidence, inclusion and positive community relationships. We believe football can be a meaningful tool for youth development and growth.
