Who We Are

About Ballan Cares

Ballan Cares (BC) is a faith-inspired non-profit community development organisation serving vulnerable children, families and communities.

Our approach is rooted in compassion, dignity, education, healthcare support, food security, empowerment and sustainable community development.

What guides us

Our Direction

Our vision and mission express the purpose behind our commitment to serving people and strengthening communities.

Our Vision

Transformed communities where vulnerable lives receive hope, dignity and opportunities to thrive.

Our Mission

To serve communities through faith-based compassion and practical solutions addressing social, spiritual and development needs.

Compassion

Serving people with care, kindness and understanding.

Dignity

Respecting every person and recognising their worth.

Practical Service

Working towards meaningful solutions to community needs.

Where we focus

Our Programme Areas

These are the areas through which Ballan Cares seeks to contribute to stronger, healthier and more hopeful communities.

Children Support

Promoting care, protection and opportunities for vulnerable children.

Education Support

Encouraging access to learning and educational opportunities.

Healthcare Support

Promoting community wellbeing through healthcare support.

Agriculture & Food Security

Encouraging sustainable agriculture and stronger food security.

Clean Water & Sanitation

Supporting the aspiration for improved access to clean water and sanitation.

Elderly Care

Promoting dignity, compassion and support for older people.

Community Empowerment

Encouraging positive participation and sustainable community development.

Youth Football Development

Using football to encourage teamwork, discipline, confidence and inclusion.

Growing through sport

Ballan Cares Youth Football Initiative

Through football, we encourage young people to develop teamwork, discipline, confidence, inclusion and positive community relationships.

We believe football can be a meaningful tool for youth development and personal growth.
