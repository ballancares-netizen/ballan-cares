Who We Are

Compassion expressed through practical action.

Ballan Cares is a faith-inspired community development organisation committed to serving vulnerable children, families and communities.

We believe every person deserves dignity, care, hope and opportunities to build a better future. Our approach is grounded in compassion, service and practical community solutions.

Through our areas of commitment, we seek to contribute to positive and sustainable change in the communities we serve.

Our Vision, Mission & Values

The purpose and principles that guide Ballan Cares.

Our Vision

Transformed communities where vulnerable lives receive hope, dignity and opportunities to thrive.

Our Mission

To serve communities through faith-based compassion and practical solutions addressing social, spiritual and development needs.

Our Values

Compassion, faith, dignity, service, inclusion, responsibility and commitment to positive change.

Youth Football

Using football to encourage teamwork, discipline, confidence, inclusion and positive development.

Ballan Cares Youth Football Initiative

Football is an opportunity to bring young people together and encourage positive growth beyond the playing field.

Through this initiative, Ballan Cares aims to encourage boys and girls to develop teamwork, discipline, confidence, inclusion and respect while enjoying the game.

We believe every young person deserves an opportunity to learn, participate and grow.
