About Ballan Cares

Ballan Cares (BC) is a faith-inspired community development organisation serving vulnerable children, families, and communities through compassion, education, healthcare support, food security, empowerment, and sustainable development initiatives.

We believe that every person deserves dignity, care, hope, and opportunities to thrive.

Our Vision

Transformed communities where vulnerable lives receive hope, dignity, and opportunities to thrive.

Our Mission

To serve communities through faith-based compassion and practical solutions addressing social, spiritual, and development needs.

Our Programmes

Ballan Cares focuses on the following programme areas:

  • Children Support: Supporting vulnerable children through care, protection, and opportunities.
  • Education: Promoting access to learning and educational opportunities.
  • Healthcare Support: Supporting community health and wellbeing.
  • Agriculture and Food Security: Encouraging sustainable food production and stronger livelihoods.
  • Clean Water: Working towards improved access to safe water.
  • Elderly Care: Promoting dignity, care, and support for older people.
  • Community Empowerment: Encouraging participation, skills, and sustainable community development.
  • Youth Football: Encouraging young people through football, teamwork, discipline, and confidence.

Ballan Cares Youth Football Initiative

The Ballan Cares Youth Football Initiative uses football as a way to encourage young people to develop teamwork, discipline, confidence, and positive community relationships.

We are starting small and growing step by step, with a commitment to creating a positive and inclusive environment for young players.

We welcome prayers, encouragement, and genuine support as this initiative develops.

Get Involved

Every act of compassion can make a difference. You can support the vision of Ballan Cares by sharing our work, keeping our programmes in your prayers, offering encouragement, or exploring appropriate opportunities to contribute.
